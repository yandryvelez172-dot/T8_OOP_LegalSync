# CodeLex-commits-T1-OOP-LegalSync-

We are "CodeLex" and We are going to do a Legal Case Management System for law firms.

Team CodeLex LegalSync

Our project will help the law firm to improve daily administration and case traceability by replacing manual spreadsheets that cause bottlenecks and delays. Currently, the lack of an active alert system creates a high risk of missing mandatory judicial deadlines, and operational fragmentation makes it difficult to link trial data with its respective evidence. Our project aims to solve this issue by developing a centralized digital hub that brings together client details, defendant records, and real-time case statuses. It includes a robust document management module, an automated calendar system that sends proactive push notifications to lawyers' mobile devices, and a global search engine to ensure timely submissions and organize legal documentation.
